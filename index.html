How could TransitCo make every trip alert more reliable—and cheaper?

WhatsApp Business Platform proposal covering channel strategy, fallback logic, solution architecture, pilot design and commercial modelling.

The problem

Push notifications can be blocked by app settings or battery restrictions, while SMS depends on telecom network availability. A missed trip alert can lead to driver waiting time, rider no-shows and avoidable support calls.

The proposed approach

Use WhatsApp as the primary channel for opted-in riders, trigger SMS if delivery is not confirmed within two minutes, and keep push running in parallel. Safety alerts go out across all channels. The proposal also maps trip moments to utility templates and outlines consent, webhook and orchestration requirements.

Business case & validation

The model estimates approximately ₹0.84 Cr lower monthly messaging spend and a further approximately ₹0.8 Cr in avoided support costs. These are illustrative estimates, not realised results. An eight-week, 50,000-rider A/B pilot is proposed to validate reach, calls, no-shows, opt-in and cost per delivered alert.

Deliverable: Commercial proposal and implementation roadmap for a January 2027 pilot. View proposal (PPTX)

How do you turn a vague Founder’s Office brief into an executable growth plan?

Brysk case study combining investor prioritisation, segment selection and a 90-day B2B demand-generation model.

Fundraising strategy

Prioritise investors by thesis fit (AI, computer vision, edge hardware and IoT), stage fit, geography (US, India and Canada) and relevant portfolio proof. The output is a ranked target list with personalised first-touch and follow-up outreach.

Demand-generation wedge

Focus on purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA) operators. The buyer is central operations or property leadership at multi-property operators. The value hypothesis is convenient on-site retail with less staffing and measurable shrinkage.

Funnel & learning loop

The planning model starts with 50 target operators and 500 outbound touches over 90 days, then hypothesises approximately 50 qualified conversations, 25 first meetings and 10 pilots. Conversion rates are assumptions to validate. Track where the funnel breaks and define in advance what would trigger a pivot in buyer, offer or channel.

Key takeaway: Research should change prioritisation, funnel maths should be treated as a hypothesis, and pivot criteria should be defined before they are needed. View case study (PDF)
